## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
* 1st Google search: *Helpful chemistry apps*
* The 1st website looked into: [Education in Chemistry](https://edu.rsc.org/ideas/eight-great-apps-for-chemistry-teachers/2010025.article)
* After reviewing the website, I found out that it's pretty useful because it guides you and tells you information about other useful apps about chemistry.
  * This website gives 8 useful apps and resources that helps you why chemistry can save the world.
* Periodic table, which gives you significant information about the element itself.
  * Example: 8 (O) Oxygen 16 which the 8 is the atomic number, O is the oxygen's symbol, and 16 is the atomic mass.
* Elemental, a simple tool for drawing chemical structures and reactions.
  * How it's useful: it does it's job accurately and perfectly. You can also save email the structures you draw.
* Tindarapo, a useful simulation to see how atoms effect on pressure.
  * How it's useful, the simulation helps us understand how atoms works on particle theory, and changing the volume changes the result of atoms.
* Chemspider, this website can help students and people who wants to learn about chemical compounds.
  * Why this website helps, it improves students and learners about how chemical compound function.
* 3D molecules edit & test, this makes students draw and view the structures as 3D to analyze them.
   * How it's useful, it makes their thinking and creativity more accurate and better to brainstorm more ideas.
* Chemist – Virtual Chem Lab, this is a fun experiment to express and has a lab report to share with others.
